--- SLIDE 1: TITLE ---
Title: "SentinelMail AI"
Subtitle: "Email Threat Detection & Forensic Intelligence Platform"
Tagline: "Not just 'Phishing — Risk 93/100.' Proof: why, where from, and how we know."
Footer: "Smart India Hackathon · SIH26106"

--- SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM ---
Headline: "Security teams triage phishing reports manually, with no evidence trail."
Bullets:
- Employees forward suspicious emails; analysts open raw headers by hand
- No consistent scoring, no chain of custody, no way to see repeat campaigns
- "Where did this come from?" is usually answered with a guess, not evidence
- Existing tools give a verdict with no explanation an analyst can defend

--- SLIDE 3: WHAT SENTINELMAIL DOES ---
Headline: "Two questions, both answered with evidence"
Two-column layout:
Column 1 — "Is it malicious?": deterministic rule engines + two independently
trained ML models + explainable 0-100 risk fusion, every point traceable to a
named finding.
Column 2 — "Where did it come from?": Earliest Reliable External Origin IP with
stated confidence and reasoning, ASN/ISP/hosting classification, reverse DNS,
DNS/WHOIS intelligence — never a guess, always labeled "unavailable" when
evidence is insufficient.

--- SLIDE 4: THE PIPELINE ---
Headline: "One pipeline, from raw bytes to verified evidence"
Show as a vertical flow diagram with icons at each stage:
Evidence Preservation (SHA-256 hash, immutable storage) → Forensic Parsing
(RFC5322/MIME + Outlook .msg) → Header Authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) →
Received-Hop Reconstruction → Earliest Reliable Origin → IOC Extraction
(domains/IPs/URLs/attachments) → Threat Intelligence Enrichment → IP/DNS/WHOIS
Forensics → Dual ML Models + Rule Engines → Risk Fusion → Campaign/Graph
Correlation → Blockchain Evidence Anchor → HTML/PDF Forensic Report
Caption: "Every stage degrades gracefully — a failed lookup never fails the case."

--- SLIDE 5: THREE WAYS IN ---
Headline: "Meets the reporter where they are"
Three cards:
1. "Human Upload" — drag-and-drop dashboard or direct API call, JWT-authenticated
2. "API Key Integration" — security tools and mail gateways post emails
   programmatically, no human login required, attributed to a dedicated
   service account
3. "Forward-to-Mailbox" — employees forward suspicious mail to a monitored
   inbox; a scheduled IMAP worker polls it, extracts the true original message
   even from "forward as attachment" wrapping, and creates a case automatically

--- SLIDE 6: DUAL AI MODELS — HONEST BY DESIGN ---
Headline: "Two models. Two different kinds of evidence. Neither is a black box."
Table/comparison:
Model 1 "Structural" — trained on header/authentication-failure patterns
Model 2 "Text Content" — TF-IDF + Logistic Regression trained on ~82,000 REAL
labeled emails from public research corpora (Enron, phishing corpora, spam
corpora)
Caption: "Every model prediction states its own training provenance in plain
language — nothing is presented as more certain than it is."

--- SLIDE 7: FORENSIC INFRASTRUCTURE INTELLIGENCE ---
Headline: "Attribution, without overclaiming"
Bullets, each paired with a small confidence-percentage badge icon:
- Earliest Reliable External Origin IP — with stated confidence, never a guess
- ASN / ISP / Hosting classification — "hosting/datacenter infrastructure,"
  never "the attacker used a VPN" without evidence
- Reverse DNS (PTR) and sender-infrastructure consistency checks
- Passive DNS (A/MX/NS/TXT) and on-demand WHOIS domain-age intelligence
- Geolocation shown with an explicit disclaimer: registered/observed network
  location, not a physical location claim
Callout box: "If it can't be determined, the system says so — it never
fabricates a plausible-looking answer."

--- SLIDE 8: EVIDENCE INTEGRITY & CHAIN OF CUSTODY ---
Headline: "Tamper-evident by construction"
Bullets:
- SHA-256 hash of every raw email computed before any parsing begins
- Blockchain-style cryptographic hash chain anchors every case's evidence hash
  — each entry cryptographically commits to the previous one
- Any alteration anywhere in the history is detectable on verification
- No email content, bodies, or attachments ever leave the hash — only hashes
- Full case status history and audit log, append-only
Small footnote: "A hash chain for tamper-evidence, not a public distributed
ledger — the honest, right-sized version of 'blockchain' for this problem."
